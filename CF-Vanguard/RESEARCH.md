# Research notes: 3DS story and card era

## Findings

- **Ride to Victory!!** (FuRyu, Nintendo 3DS, 2013) includes more than 1,000 cards through Booster Set 9 (BT09), tutorials, six original player avatars, and a story covering the first two anime seasons. Its original fighter starts at Card Capital and advances through shop, regional, and national tournaments. BT09 is the Limit Break / Ultimate Break and crossride era.
- **Lock on Victory!!** (FuRyu, Nintendo 3DS, 2014) continues the 3DS series and expands into the Link Joker season. Its story has three school routes: Hitsue High (Kai), Fukuhara High (Ren), and Miyaji Academy (Aichi). The central high-school tournament is VF Koshien; the Link Joker / Reverse conflict becomes the later story arc.
- Its period card pool includes the earlier cards plus Break Ride and Lock. Community references place the top of the pool around BT15 / EB10; I treat that as an era guide, since the Japanese and English releases and promotional cards do not line up exactly. The Link Joker invasion begins with BT12's Link Joker and Reverse units; BT15 includes Star-vader, “Omega” Glendios.
- The old format uses a 50-card deck including a Grade 0 first Vanguard, grade-up normal rides of the same or next grade, calls limited by Vanguard grade, grade-limited guarding, Grade 3 Twin Drive, and matching-clan trigger checks. Limit Break 4 is active at four or more damage. Break Ride is a Limit Break 4 ability that activates when a same-clan unit rides over the unit and grants +10,000 power plus a card-specific effect. Lock turns a rear-guard face down and inactive until it unlocks at the end of its owner's turn.
- The local catalog now contains 2,083 unique English card entries and matching images from the official English card-list service, filtered to BT01-BT15, EB01-EB10, and TD01-TD13. It supplies the library and the sample decks' names, clans, grades, power, shield, trigger kind, and card art. This is a broad public card pool, not a complete inventory of Japanese-only, promotional, or game-exclusive cards.
- The deck builder assembles 50-card sample decks from that catalog and observes a four-copy ceiling for its grade 1/2 groups and trigger groups. It does not reproduce exact 3DS opponent lists or all effects. The project uses the public card service and documented rules; it does not extract commercial game binaries or ROM assets.
